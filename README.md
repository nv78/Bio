# Bio

# Natan Vidra

**Occupation:** Entrepreneur, artificial intelligence researcher  
**Known for:** Founder of Anote  
**Education:** Cornell University

**Natan Vidra** is an entrepreneur and artificial intelligence researcher known as the co-founder and chief executive officer of the artificial intelligence company **Anote**.

His work focuses on artificial intelligence systems, including machine learning, human-in-the-loop learning systems, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and evaluation of large language models.

---

## Career

Vidra is the co-founder and CEO of **Anote**, an applied artificial intelligence research company based in New York City.

Prior to founding Anote, Vidra worked as a data scientist and software engineer within Deloitte's Applied AI division, where he contributed to projects involving natural language processing, computer vision, and data analytics.

---

## Education

Vidra graduated from Cornell University with a **Bachelor of Science in Electrical and Computer Engineering** and a **Master of Engineering in Computer Science**.

During his time at Cornell, he worked on a variety of artificial intelligence research and engineering projects including robotics, machine learning systems, and generative AI experiments.

---

## Research

Vidra has co-authored research papers related to human-in-the-loop machine learning and retrieval-augmented generation systems.

### Improving Classification Performance with Human Feedback

Vidra co-authored the paper:

**Improving Classification Performance with Human Feedback: Label a Few, We Label the Rest**

The paper proposes a framework for improving classification accuracy using limited human-labeled data.

Paper:  
https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.09555

---

### Enhancing Large Language Model Performance to Answer Questions and Extract Information More Accurately

Vidra also co-authored research exploring techniques for improving the performance of large language models in question answering and information extraction tasks.

Paper:  
https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.01722

---

### Improving Retrieval for RAG-Based Question Answering Models on Financial Documents

Vidra contributed to research on improving retrieval methods in retrieval-augmented generation systems for financial document analysis.

Paper:  
https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.07221
